The top line of figures for each subject are the National GCSE grade percentages for 2025 followed by those for previous years.
The results for 2021 and 2020 were based on teacher grades, rather than examinations, because of the Covid-19 virus.
Where subjects appear to be missing result figures this will often be because the naming or classification of the subject has changed. For 2018 a number of subjects were no longer offered for examination.
The 2018 exam system changed radically making year on year comparison difficult. For example, Northern Ireland schools had a choice of which grading system to follow so subject numbers may vary considerably from last year. The figures for 2025 are only provisional as of August 2025.

The 2019 figures and thereafter are for England only, NOT the UK as a whole as previously, and all ages.
I have therefore included the 2018 figures again, but for England only, for comparison with 2019. They may be similar to the 2018 figures for the UK but are for slightly different populations.
