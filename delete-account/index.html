Account

Delete your account

We're sorry to see you go. Here's exactly how to permanently remove your Lockin.tech account and all associated data.

1

Email our support team

Send a deletion request to support@lockin.tech from the email address linked to your account.

2

Include your account details

Add your account username or email address so we can locate your account. We may need to verify your identity before proceeding.

3

We respond within 90 days

In most cases we'll action your request much sooner — but under UK and Gibraltar GDPR we are required to respond within 90 days at the latest.

4

Your data is permanently removed

Once your account is deleted, all your data — including study progress, notes and preferences — is permanently and irreversibly removed from our systems.

Ready to proceed? Click below to start your deletion request.
